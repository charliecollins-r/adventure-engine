# The Taxidermist — Scenario Documentation

## Story Synopsis

You awaken on a cold steel table in the basement of a remote Appalachian cabin. Above, heavy boots pace the floorboards and a man hums to country music. The walls are lined with preserved animals — an impressive collection that hints at the skill of their creator. But some of the poses are too human, and the glass eyes seem to follow you.

You're small — a goblin-sized creature barely a third of Emmett's height. The workbench looms above you, the mounted animals on the upper walls are at your scale or larger, and every tool is oversized in your hands. Your size is a double-edged blade: you can squeeze beneath the workbench, slip behind the shelving units, and fit inside spaces Emmett would never think to search — but reaching anything above waist-height on a human means climbing, stacking, or improvising.

Emmett Craw is a reclusive taxidermist, mid-50s, soft-spoken and folksy. He's graduated from preserving animals to preserving people. He genuinely believes he's "preserving beauty" — a calling he inherited from his dead mother, whom he references constantly. His workshop is both his studio and your prison.

The basement contains a main work area with a steel table, workbench, tool pegboard, specimen displays, a furnace, and storage shelves. A chemical shelf holds the dangerous supplies that Emmett uses in his craft. A storm cellar with padlocked doors and iron bars is your way out — if you can get through both. A workbench closeup reveals his notebook, tools, and the secrets of his trade.

**Difficulty**: Hard | **Estimated Time**: 15 minutes | **Patterns Used**: 25+

> **Note**: The antagonist mechanic is optional. This scenario works as a pure point-and-click adventure if the antagonist AI is disabled in the scenario configuration.

## Game Flow

### Phase 1: Trapped
You start strapped to the steel table. Emmett checks every 70s with a high noise threshold (60). He's relaxed, content, humming along to music upstairs.

**Immediate goals**: Free yourself from the table.
1. **Hand the table** — struggle against straps; after a delay, the weakened left buckle gives way (noise: 15)
2. **Take leather strap piece** — remnant of the restraint becomes a tool
3. **Explore the workbench** — find notebook, skinning knife, magnifying lens

### Phase 2: Working
Triggered when freed from straps. Emmett's interval drops to 50s, threshold to 45. He's noticed less noise from below.

**Three major escape chains**:

**Chain A — Mama's Recipe (Chemical Escape)**:
- Read notebook → learn cabinet combination (4-7-2-8) and lye formula
- Enter code on cabinet lock → get rubber gloves, lye powder, acetone
- Combine lye + acetone (wearing gloves) → create lye mixture
- Find specimen key inside mounted coyote (cut it open with skinning knife)
- Use specimen key on cellar padlock
- Apply lye mixture to iron bars → weaken them
- Push through weakened bars → escape

**Chain B — Hacksaw Justice (Tool Escape)**:
- Find hacksaw blade on tool pegboard
- Combine hacksaw blade + leather strap → handled hacksaw
- Use hacksaw on padlock → cut it off
- Use hacksaw on iron bars → saw through all three
- Turn on radio for sound masking during sawing
- Push through → escape

**Chain C — Burn It Down (Fire Escape)**:
- Take coal chunk from furnace, gather kindling from shelves
- Take matches from storage shelves
- Get formaldehyde from chemical shelf (need code or smash glass)
- Combine formaldehyde + kindling → accelerant bundle
- Use accelerant on furnace, light it → intense fire weakens stone wall
- Kick through weakened wall → escape

### Phase 3: Pursuit
Triggered when Emmett becomes suspicious (missing knife, cabinet opened, fire detected). Interval drops to 25s, threshold to 20. He's coming downstairs with purpose.

- **Evidence Awareness**: Emmett notices if the skinning knife is missing, if the cabinet has been opened, or if fire is detected. Each triggers suspicion escalation.

## Killer Profile: Emmett Craw

**Background**: Reclusive Appalachian taxidermist, mid-50s. Learned the craft from his mother and has practiced it for decades. Graduated from preserving animals to preserving people — he genuinely believes he's "preserving beauty." References his dead mother ("Mama") constantly as his moral authority. Lives alone in a remote cabin surrounded by forest.

**Personality**: Soft-spoken, folksy, patient. Uses terms of endearment ("darlin'," "sweetheart"). The horror comes from the disconnect between his gentle manner and his intentions. He hums country music while working. When angry, he drops the sweetness and Mama's voice comes through — cold, commanding, judgmental.

**Base Behavior**:
- Phase 1 (Trapped): Check every 70s, threshold 60 (relaxed, humming)
- Phase 2 (Working): Check every 50s, threshold 45 (concerned, listening)
- Phase 3 (Pursuit): Check every 25s, threshold 20 (determined, dangerous)
- Thoroughness: 0.35 → 0.75 with evidence

**Dialogue Samples**:
- Approach: "Now then, let me check on my new project..." / "Don't you fret, darlin'. Emmett's comin'."
- Search: "Now where'd you get to, sweetheart?" / "I ain't mad. I just need you back on the table, is all."
- Evidence: "Well now. Somebody's been busy, ain't they?" / "Now who told you you could touch my things?"
- Catch: "There you are. Now come on back to the table. We got work to do, you and me." / "Shh shh shh. It's alright. It'll all be over soon. You're gonna be beautiful."

**Special Behaviors**:
- `distracted_by_visitor`: When the visitor event fires (360s), Emmett goes upstairs for an extended period — major action window
- `fire_panic`: Fire triggers immediate pursuit phase; Emmett becomes erratic and aggressive
- `enraged_mode`: After multiple evidence discoveries, Emmett abandons stealth and actively hunts

**On-Visit Effects**:
- Open chemical cabinet → Emmett relocks it and becomes suspicious
- Missing skinning knife → Emmett flags the absence, heightened alertness
- Fire detected → Immediate pursuit phase, Emmett panics
