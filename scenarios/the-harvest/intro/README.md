# Intro cinematic assets (The Harvest)

The brief with every Gemini and ElevenLabs prompt: https://claude.ai/artifact/FJ2V2J4NJD89cLv8xPueE3

- `reference/`: single frames of the in-game player (on grey, 2x) to attach to every sprite prompt.
- `backgrounds/`: the six intro paintings (1920x1080).
- `sprites/raw/`: raw Gemini sprite frames (base + edits), keyed and assembled into `sprites/` by a script.
- `audio/`: narration, radio, music and sound effects.
