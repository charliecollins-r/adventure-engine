# The Harvest — Scenario Documentation

## Story Synopsis

You are a deep-space salvage operator aboard the *Outrider*, a one-person recovery vessel working the fringes of explored space. You responded to an automated distress beacon — standard salvage protocol. What you found was not standard. A massive alien vessel, kilometers long, organic and mechanical in equal measure, drifting in orbit above an uncharted planet. You docked. You boarded. You blacked out.

You wake in a stasis pod — one of hundreds, stacked in a cavernous hold like specimens in a collection jar. Most pods hold dead things: creatures from dozens of worlds, preserved in amber-like fluid. Some are fresh. The ship belongs to **The Choir** — tall, eyeless bio-mechanical drones that communicate through subsonic resonance. They are harvesters. Worker-bees serving an ancient intelligence called **The Convergence** — an entity so old it predates most stars. The Convergence consumes the energy of planets. It arrives, seeds its infrastructure, drains the world to a husk, and moves on. It has done this across galaxies for eons. The Choir are its hands: they collect specimens before each harvest (cataloging biodiversity the way a logger might photograph a forest before clear-cutting it), maintain the harvesting infrastructure, and eliminate interference.

Your pod malfunctioned. You're loose on a ship full of things that want you back in the jar. Below you, a planet is being consumed alive — its former civilization already reduced to empty ruins and warped echoes. And somewhere on that dying world, those former inhabitants built something to fight back. They failed. But their weapon is still down there.

Five acts. Fifty views. Thirty patterns. Multiple endings — from selfish escape to galactic sacrifice. Items found in the first hour are the key to survival in the tenth. Every choice matters. Some close doors forever.

**Difficulty**: Extreme | **Estimated Time**: 8–10 hours | **Patterns Used**: 30 (all, many multiple) | **Flagship scenario** — 50 views, ~180 objects, ~68 items (50 base + 18 combinations), 6 endings

## Game Flow

### Act 1: Specimen (~90 minutes)

**Phase**: `awakening` — Patrol interval 90s, threshold 70 (The Curator is distant, performing maintenance)

You wake in the stasis hold. Your pod malfunctioned. You're disoriented, weak, and alone among the dead and the dying.

**Immediate goals**:
1. **Search your broken pod** — find stasis fluid sample (key item for Act 5 combination)
2. **Study adjacent pods** — learn about the Choir's collection methods, find a shard of pod glass (improvised tool)
3. **Access the processing lab** — discover what happens to specimens, find surgical tools
4. **Navigate containment corridors** — avoid The Curator on its rounds
5. **Reach the security hub** — gain intel on ship layout, disable local containment locks
6. **Find the specimen archive** — learn about The Convergence and its history
7. **Reach the airlock bay** — discover your ship is gone; find emergency suit components

**Key items found**:
- Stasis fluid vial (Act 1 → used in Act 5 combination)
- Pod glass shard (improvised cutting tool)
- Surgical probe (alien tool, multiple uses)
- Containment key card (access card, functions on ship only)
- Data crystal — personal log (translation cipher fragment 1/4)
- Nutrient paste tube (consumable, used for distraction)
- Specimen tag (your own — identity proof for Act 5)

**Act 1 → Act 2 transition**: Escape through ventilation shaft to operational decks, or hack the security hub to unlock the inter-deck elevator.

---

### Act 2: The Hive (~120 minutes)

**Phase**: `infiltration` — Patrol interval 60s, threshold 50 (The Curator is actively searching; Maintenance Drones added)

The operational decks are the Choir's domain. The architecture shifts from clinical to lived-in alien. Everything is designed for beings that don't think like you.

**Immediate goals**:
1. **Navigate the bridge** — learn the ship's mission, see the harvesting operation, find star charts
2. **Access engineering** — obtain power cell for the damaged shuttle in the shuttle bay
3. **Reach the comm array** — find ship's black box recorder (key item for Act 5 ally path)
4. **Explore the armory** — find a resonance disruptor (weapon/tool against Choir tech)
5. **Enter the hive quarters** — find translation cipher fragment 2/4 in the resonance chamber
6. **Reach the shuttle bay** — repair a shuttle for planetfall
7. **Use the data core** — decrypt Convergence communications, learn about the planet below

**Key items found**:
- Ship's black box (Act 2 → used in Act 5 to prove sentience to Curator)
- Power cell (repair the shuttle)
- Resonance disruptor (weapon/tool, disrupts Choir locks and mechanisms)
- Navigation chip (required for shuttle launch; also Act 5 escape ending)
- Translation cipher fragment 2/4
- Choir resonance crystal (Act 2 → Act 5 countermeasure component)
- Bio-garden extract (combined with other ingredients for Act 5)
- Comm array schematics (needed for Act 5 signal ending)

**Combination crafting (Act 2)**:
- Pod glass shard + Bio-garden fiber = Reinforced blade
- Nutrient paste + Stasis fluid = Sedative compound (delay Choir drones)
- Containment key card + Resonance disruptor = Universal bypass (opens any ship lock)

**Act 2 → Act 3 transition**: Launch the repaired shuttle toward the planet. The Curator tries to stop you — this is the first true timed escape sequence. Success means planetfall. Failure means recapture and a harder second attempt.

---

### Act 3: Descent (~90 minutes)

**Phase**: `survival` — Patrol interval 45s, threshold 40 (Echoes — remnants of the consumed civilization, manifesting from the Convergence's energy. They don't patrol — they trigger from proximity to emotionally charged locations)

The planet's surface is a wasteland in slow-motion death. The Convergence is still actively draining it. The former civilization's ruins dot the landscape.

**Immediate goals**:
1. **Salvage the crashed shuttle** — retrieve supplies and navigation equipment
2. **Cross the wasteland** — find safe routes between landmarks
3. **Investigate the siphon tower** — understand the harvesting process up close
4. **Explore the refugee camp** — find records of the evacuation, translation cipher fragment 3/4
5. **Rest at the fungal grove** — safe haven, crafting location, resupply
6. **Navigate the echo field** — dangerous but contains key information
7. **Discover the military bunker** — find alien weapons and the planet's final battle records
8. **Reach the city gates** — enter the dead city

**Key items found**:
- Shuttle emergency beacon (can recall shuttle to a safe location for escape ending)
- Wasteland mineral sample (combined with stasis fluid for dampener in Act 5)
- Siphon crystal fragment (broken off the tower — raw Convergence energy)
- Refugee journal (translation cipher fragment 3/4)
- Fungal extract (healing/crafting ingredient)
- Echo stabilizer (alien tech from the bunker — lets you interact with Echoes safely)
- Alien sidearm (weapon from the bunker)
- Bunker defense schematics (partial weapon plans)

**Combination crafting (Act 3)**:
- Stasis fluid vial + Wasteland mineral = Energy dampener (Act 5 — suppresses Convergence energy locally)
- Siphon crystal + Resonance disruptor = Overcharge device (used to overload systems)
- Fungal extract + Sedative compound = Neural suppressant (makes you invisible to Echoes briefly)

**Act 3 → Act 4 transition**: Enter the dead city through the gates. The city's automated defenses activate when they detect non-native biology — you need the echo stabilizer to register as a "memory" of a former inhabitant.

---

### Act 4: The Dead City (~120 minutes)

**Phase**: `investigation` — Patrol interval 40s, threshold 35 (Echoes are more common in the city; **The Watcher** appears — a Convergence construct that monitors the ruins, the first sign the Convergence itself is aware of you)

The dead city is liminal horror at its peak. Everything is preserved, abandoned mid-moment. The Echoes here are more coherent — some can almost communicate. The city holds the key to fighting back.

**Immediate goals**:
1. **Explore the grand plaza** — orientation, find city map, encounter first coherent Echo
2. **Research the library** — find the civilization's studies on the Convergence, translation cipher fragment 4/4
3. **Infiltrate the war room** — learn the final battle plan, discover the weapon existed
4. **Access the research lab** — find weapon schematics, understand the countermeasure theory
5. **Search the residential block** — find personal items that unlock emotional Echoes with key information
6. **Restore the power station** — bring the city's systems back online (enables other actions)
7. **Visit the temple** — learn the civilization's prophecy about "the one who comes after"
8. **Navigate the underground railway** — fast travel between city districts
9. **Survive the echo chamber** — confront a time-fracture, learn a key revelation
10. **Open the weapon vault** — obtain countermeasure components

**Key items found**:
- City keystone (access to restricted areas)
- Translation cipher fragment 4/4 (complete the cipher → read all alien text)
- Weapon core (the countermeasure's heart — key Act 5 component)
- Defense grid controller (can reactivate the city's orbital defense satellites)
- Alien personal locket (emotional item, triggers key Echo interaction)
- Power cell adapter (bridges human/alien tech — needed to interface with multiple systems)
- Prophet's crystal (from the temple — prophetic visions of possible futures / endings)
- Railway pass token (enables underground rail travel)

**Combination crafting (Act 4)**:
- All 4 translation cipher fragments = Complete translation cipher (read all alien text in the game)
- Weapon core + Choir resonance crystal = Primed weapon core (partially assembled countermeasure)
- Power cell adapter + Navigation chip = Universal interface (interact with any alien tech)
- Overcharge device + Defense grid controller = Orbital weapon (one-shot, devastating — kamikaze ending path)

**Act 4 → Act 5 transition**: Use the underground railway's deepest tunnel, which leads beneath the city toward the nexus. Or follow the Echo of a scientist who shows you the path they took during the assault. The transition triggers the final phase and a point of no return.

---

### Act 5: The Nexus (~120 minutes)

**Phase**: `endgame` — Patrol interval 25s, threshold 20 (Everything is hunting you: Echoes, The Watcher, Convergence defense systems. The Curator has followed you from orbit — it crash-landed a shuttle to pursue you. Depending on your choices, it may be hostile or a reluctant ally.)

The Convergence nexus is overwhelming. Reality bends around it. The energy of an entire planet flows through these crystalline halls. This is the final test.

**Goals depend on chosen ending path** (multiple can be pursued simultaneously until the point of commitment):

1. **Navigate the energy conduits** — find safe paths through lethal energy flows
2. **Reach the convergence chamber** — the planet-killing machine at the heart
3. **Access the control spire** — where the harvesting can be redirected or stopped
4. **Discover the stasis gallery** — the Convergence's trophy room (optional horror/lore)
5. **Set up at the weapon assembly point** — where the former civilization staged their assault
6. **Reach the transmission array** — for the signal ending
7. **Confront the core** — face the Convergence intelligence itself
8. **Find the escape route** — the shaft to the surface for escape endings

**Key items found**:
- Convergence energy sample (pure, incredibly dangerous)
- Ancient access key (opens deeper nexus sections)
- Curator's signal device (taken from the Curator — calls the ship)
- Core interface module (allows direct communication with the Convergence)

**Final combination crafting (Act 5)**:
- Primed weapon core + Energy dampener + Siphon crystal fragment = **The Countermeasure** (fully assembled weapon that can destroy the nexus)
- Comm array schematics + Universal interface + Transmission array components = **Galactic Broadcast** (signal ending setup)
- Ship's black box + Specimen tag + Curator's signal device = **Proof of sentience** (convinces the Curator to stand down / assist)
- Bio-garden extract + Convergence energy sample + Core interface module = **Neural bridge** (interface with the Convergence directly — dark ending)

## Endings (6 Paths)

### Ending 1: Escape — "The Survivor" (Selfish)
**Requirements**: Navigation chip, shuttle emergency beacon, escape shaft key, and either a working shuttle OR the Curator's cooperation.

You flee. You recall your shuttle (or commandeer the Curator's), navigate out of the system, and jump to charted space. The planet dies behind you. The Convergence continues. You survive with the knowledge of what's out there — but you do nothing with it.

**Emotional tone**: Hollow victory. You lived, but at what cost? A cutscene shows the planet going dark as you fly away.

**Steps**: ~14 (moderate difficulty, requires items from Acts 1-3 and Act 5 escape route)

### Ending 2: Destroy — "The Sacrifice" (Heroic)
**Requirements**: The Countermeasure (assembled from items across Acts 1-5), ancient access key, ancient blueprint (to know where to place it).

You activate the countermeasure at the convergence chamber's heart. The nexus overloads and collapses, severing the Convergence's hold on this planet. The energy release is cataclysmic. You don't make it out. The planet begins to heal. The Choir ship, cut off from its master, goes dormant.

**Emotional tone**: Noble sacrifice. The hardest ending to achieve but the most complete victory. The planet lives.

**Steps**: ~20 (hardest — requires items from all 5 acts assembled in a specific order)

### Ending 3: Sabotage + Escape — "The Best of Both" (Golden)
**Requirements**: The Countermeasure + escape path items + a timer mechanism (Overcharge Device jury-rigged as a detonator).

You set the countermeasure on a timer, then race through the escape shaft to the surface and off-planet before it detonates. The nexus is destroyed AND you survive. This is the true "golden ending" — requires the most items, the most combinations, and a final timed escape sequence.

**Emotional tone**: Triumphant. Hard-earned. You beat the system.

**Steps**: ~24 (maximum difficulty — requires nearly every key item and combination in the game)

### Ending 4: Signal — "The Warning" (Legacy)
**Requirements**: Galactic Broadcast (assembled), transmission array access, the Complete Translation Cipher (to encode the message in universal formats).

You can't destroy the Convergence alone — it spans galaxies. But you can warn everyone. You hijack the nexus's own transmission array and broadcast everything: the Convergence's nature, the countermeasure schematics, a warning to every inhabited system in range. You may not survive the Convergence's retaliation, but you've armed the galaxy.

**Emotional tone**: Hopeful sacrifice. You might die, but you've given the galaxy a fighting chance.

**Steps**: ~18 (requires Acts 2 and 4 items primarily)

### Ending 5: Kamikaze — "The Hammer" (Destructive)
**Requirements**: Orbital Weapon (assembled), Curator's Signal Device (to recall the Choir ship), override access to the ship's navigation.

You recall the Choir vessel from orbit and use the planet's reactivated defense satellites to drive it straight into the nexus. The collision destroys both the ship and the nexus in a cataclysmic impact. You watch from the surface. The Convergence on this planet is obliterated. But the debris will make the surface uninhabitable for centuries.

**Emotional tone**: Pyrrhic victory. Maximum destruction. You survive but the planet doesn't — you've traded one death for another.

**Steps**: ~16 (requires specific Act 2 + Act 4 combination items)

### Ending 6: Transcendence — "The Bridge" (Dark/Ambiguous)
**Requirements**: Neural Bridge (assembled), Core Interface Module, prophet's crystal (for guidance through the merge).

You interface directly with the Convergence intelligence. You see what it sees: the universe winding down, entropy winning, the Convergence's desperate attempt to outlast heat death by consuming energy wherever it can find it. It's not evil — it's afraid. You merge with it, becoming a hybrid intelligence that can redirect its hunger. You are no longer human. But the harvesting stops — on this world, on every world. You've become something that transcends both species.

**Emotional tone**: Deeply ambiguous. Is this a win? You've saved every world the Convergence would have consumed. But you've ceased to exist as a person. The last scene is you/the Convergence looking out at the stars, feeling hunger, choosing restraint.

**Steps**: ~16 (requires Act 2, 4, and 5 items; the "easy" dark path, but emotionally the hardest)

## Killer Profiles

### The Curator (Acts 1-2, appears in Act 5)

**Background**: A specialized Choir unit responsible for the specimen collection. Taller than standard Choir drones, with additional manipulator limbs and optical sensors that glow amber. It catalogues, preserves, and maintains specimens with obsessive precision. You are its most recent acquisition — and its first escape.

**Personality**: Methodical, persistent, patient. It doesn't rush. It doesn't make mistakes. It follows patterns and routines, checking each section of its domain in sequence. When it finds evidence of your passage, it adapts its patrol route. It learns.

**Base Behavior**:
- Act 1: Check every 90s, threshold 70, thoroughness 0.2
- Act 2: Check every 60s, threshold 50, thoroughness 0.4
- Act 5 (hostile): Check every 30s, threshold 25, thoroughness 0.7
- Act 5 (ally): Does not patrol — follows you, helps with Convergence defenses

**Dialogue Samples**:
- Distant: *A subsonic hum vibrates through the walls. The Curator is somewhere on this deck, performing its rounds.*
- Approaching: *Click. Click. Click. Precise footsteps on metal grating. Amber light sweeps across the corridor ahead.*
- Searching: *The Curator's manipulator limbs unfold, probing surfaces, checking specimens. Its optical sensors pan slowly, missing nothing.*
- Evidence found: *The amber glow intensifies. The Curator pauses. Its head tilts — a mechanical gesture of recognition. It knows something is wrong.*
- Capture: *Four manipulator limbs close around you with clinical precision. No pain — it doesn't want to damage its specimen. You are carried, struggling uselessly, back to a containment cell.*

**On-Visit Effects**:
- Replaces items to their original positions
- Re-seals opened doors and vents
- Reactivates disabled security systems
- In Act 5 (hostile): Actively hunts, calls Choir drones

### Echoes (Acts 3-4, present in Act 5)

**Background**: Remnants of the planet's consumed civilization, preserved in the Convergence's energy field like images burned into a screen. They replay moments from their lives — final moments, usually. Some are coherent enough to almost communicate. Most are just loops: a figure walking through a wall, a child playing in a space that no longer exists, a soldier firing at nothing.

**Personality**: Varied. Incoherent Echoes are environmental hazards — they manifest suddenly and can be startling (raising noise). Coherent Echoes are tragic: aware enough to know they're dead, but unable to break their loop without help (Echo Stabilizer + Resonance Tuning Fork).

**Base Behavior**:
- Act 3: Check every 45s, threshold 40, thoroughness 0.3
- Act 4: Check every 40s, threshold 35, thoroughness 0.5
- Act 5: Check every 30s, threshold 25, thoroughness 0.6
- Echoes don't patrol — they trigger from proximity to emotionally significant locations

**Dialogue Samples**:
- Manifestation: *The air shimmers. A translucent figure materializes — tall, four-armed, wearing a lab coat. It doesn't see you. It's looking at something that isn't there anymore.*
- Incoherent: *The Echo stutters, its form glitching. It walks through the same three steps, turns, walks back. Again. Again. Its mouth moves but no sound comes out.*
- Coherent (with Echo Communicator): *"You... are not one of us. You are warm. Alive. How long has it been? The crystal... it took everything. We tried to stop it. Did we fail? We failed."*
- Hostile: *The Echo snaps toward you, its form distorting into something wrong — too many limbs, face stretched. A scream that sounds like static. It reaches for you.*
- Helpful: *The Echo points — deliberately, knowingly — toward a wall that looks solid. Then it walks through. On the other side: a hidden passage.*

**On-Visit Effects**:
- Manifestation raises ambient noise
- Hostile Echoes can trigger The Watcher's attention
- Coherent Echoes provide hints and directions if communicated with properly

### The Watcher (Act 4, present in Act 5)

**Background**: A Convergence defense construct. Unlike the biological Choir, The Watcher is pure crystalline energy given form. It manifests as a geometric shape — a shifting polyhedron of light that floats through the ruins, scanning for threats to the harvesting operation. It's the Convergence becoming aware that something is moving through its infrastructure.

**Personality**: Alien beyond comprehension. It doesn't think — it processes. It doesn't hunt — it optimizes. When it finds you, it doesn't capture you. It restructures reality around you: sealing exits, creating barriers, funneling you away from sensitive areas.

**Base Behavior**:
- Act 4: Check every 40s, threshold 35, thoroughness 0.5
- Act 5: Check every 25s, threshold 20, thoroughness 0.8

**Dialogue Samples**:
- Distant: *A faint geometric glow passes behind a wall. The air tastes of ozone.*
- Approaching: *Light refracts wrong. Shadows point the wrong direction. A crystalline hum builds in your teeth.*
- Scanning: *The polyhedron unfolds, faces rotating, each one a different sensor. Light beams sweep the area in mathematical patterns, covering every angle.*
- Detected: *The Watcher LOCKS. Every face orients toward you. The hum becomes a shriek. Crystal grows from the walls, sealing the doorway behind you.*
- Restructure: *The room shifts. Walls slide. A new passage opens where there wasn't one — leading away from the restricted area. The Watcher doesn't attack. It redirects.*

**On-Visit Effects**:
- Seals paths you've opened toward sensitive areas
- Creates crystal barriers that require the Energy Dampener to pass
- Alerts other Convergence defenses to your presence
- In Act 5: Directly attacks by crystallizing surfaces you touch

## Schema Considerations: Acts

The existing phase system supports act-based structure. Each act maps to a phase with `starts_when` conditions gating progression. Recommended schema additions for long-format scenarios:

- **`act`** field on phases (integer) — groups phases into acts for UI display
- **`act_title`** field — display name for the act
- **`point_of_no_return`** boolean on phase transitions — warns player before committing
- **`available_views`** on phases — restricts which views are accessible per act (prevents backtracking to completed acts)

These are backward-compatible additions that don't affect simpler scenarios.

## Image Generation Prompts — Quick Reference

All prompts above are ready to paste into **Google Gemini (free tier, Imagen 3)**. The target art style is **1990s Sierra On-Line adventure games** — specifically **King's Quest V** and **King's Quest VI**. These games featured hand-painted, lush, richly detailed backgrounds with painterly textures, atmospheric lighting, and a sense of depth and wonder even in dark or dangerous scenes.

### General Tips
- Every prompt starts with: `"Hand-painted game background art in the style of 1990s Sierra On-Line adventure games like King's Quest V and King's Quest VI"`
- Every prompt ends with: `"No text, no UI, no characters"` (unless the scene specifically needs environmental characters like the Echo)
- Add `"1920x1080"` or `"16:9 aspect ratio"` when resolution options are available
- If results look too modern/3D, append: `"painterly style, hand-painted, lush detailed backgrounds, rich colors, atmospheric, 1990s adventure game"`
- If results are too bright/cheerful for horror scenes, append: `"dark atmosphere, muted color palette, desaturated, ominous"`
- If the alien architecture looks too human, append: `"non-human architecture, biomechanical, HR Giger inspired"`
